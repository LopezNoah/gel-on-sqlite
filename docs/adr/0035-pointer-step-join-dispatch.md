# One home for the pointer-step join dispatch (scope down the "one harness" idea)

A round-5 architecture review proposed unifying the three pointer-path lowerings in `gel_ir_compiler.ts` — `tryCompileScalarPointerPathSelectSQL` (scalar leaf), `tryCompileLinkPropertyPathSelectSQL` (link-property leaf), `tryCompileCorrelatedScalarPointerPathSQL` (correlated subquery) — into a single harness that takes a leaf-projection callback.

On close reading the three are **not** near-identical end-to-end, and a single harness is not safe:

- they use different path extractors (`extractScalarPointerPath` vs `extractLinkPropertyPath` vs `buildCorrelatedScalarPointerPath`, the last already factored);
- the link-property lowering has **bespoke terminal-link handling** (the last link joins the junction table and the chain stops on the *link* alias, not a target alias);
- they compute the per-step projected columns differently (`pointerPathAliasColumns` vs an inline `id`/inline-FK `Set`);
- the leaf and result wrappers genuinely differ — `SELECT DISTINCT … WHERE leaf IS NOT NULL`, a non-`DISTINCT` link-property column, a correlated `(SELECT … WHERE anchor)`.

What **is** byte-identical, and duplicated well beyond the three functions, is the per-link **dispatch** between the two pointer-step join shapes: `shouldUseLinkTable(link) ? pointerStepJoinSql({usesLinkTable:true, …, linkAlias, linkTable}) : pointerStepJoinSql({usesLinkTable:false, …, inlineColumn})`. ADR 0011 unified the four *join shapes* behind `pointerStepJoinSql`; the *choice* between them was re-spelled at every chain-loop site (five of them: the two scalar/link-property lowerings, the correlated builder `buildCorrelatedScalarPointerPath`, the chained-correlated builder, and the polymorphic multi-step source), differing only in the bound aliases.

**Decision (done):** extract `pointerStepJoinForLink(link, previousAlias, nextAlias, targetSource, linkAlias, options)` — the one home for that dispatch. It recomputes `linkTable` via `linkTableNameForPointer` in the link-table arm (identical to every call site's value) and uses the invariant `` `${link.ptrref.shortName}_id` `` in the inline arm. The five sites collapse from a ~17-line ternary each to a single call (`gel_ir_compiler.ts` –48 lines net). The leaf projections, the link-property terminal handling, and the correlated anchors stay with their callers — they are not shared.

**Decision (scoped out, deliberately):** the **leaf-callback "one harness" is not built.** The three lowerings diverge at the extractor, the terminal link, the column strategy, and the result wrapper; folding them behind one callback-driven builder would reconcile four independent axes on the SQL hot path, where a change surfaces as wrong rows. The dispatch is the identical, low-risk piece — extracted now; the harness is not worth its risk. (Same reasoning ADR 0007 used for the ORDER BY comparators and ADR 0014 for the full `evalExpr` move.)

**Verification.** Byte-identical SQL, proven two ways. (1) The deterministic golden + pointer-heavy conformance set (`inspect`, `inspect_corpus`, `sql_query`, `sql_parse`, `pointer_join`, `edgeql_{select,linkprops,linkatoms,advtypes,group}`, 1147 tests) ran `206 failed / 532 passed / 409 skipped` both before and after, with **identical per-file failure counts** (advtypes 62, group 4, linkatoms 15, linkprops 49, select 82) — zero movement among the 532 passing pointer-path result assertions. (2) Direct canonical-SQL diff (`bin/inspect.ts sql …`) for a scalar chain (`Issue.owner.name`), a link-property (`Issue.owner@since`), a correlated aggregate (`count(Issue.owner.name)`), and computed-shape pointers (`Issue { .owner.name }`, `Issue { count(.owner.name) }`) was empty — byte-for-byte identical. `tsc` clean. The helper's behaviour is the join-shape choice it delegates to `pointerStepJoinSql`, which keeps its own unit surface (`tests/pointer_join.test.ts`); the dispatch is verified by the byte-identical SQL above, so no redundant unit test is added.

**Consequences.** The pointer-step join *dispatch* has one home alongside the join *shapes* (`pointerStepJoinSql`) and the *alias scheme* (ADR 0022). A sixth chain-loop site can no longer get the dispatch args subtly wrong, and the two join shapes can evolve in one place.

**Why record it:** a future reader will see `pointerStepJoinForLink` used by five chain loops and the three path lowerings still separate, and may think the "one harness" candidate was abandoned. It was scoped to the byte-identical dispatch deliberately — the three lowerings differ materially at the leaf, terminal, and columns; the harness is the part that was *not* safe to unify.
